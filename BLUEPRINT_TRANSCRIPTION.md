# MAP Grade-Level Blueprint Transcription

Status date: **2026-08-23**

This document records the current Missouri DESE MAP Grade-Level blueprint values used as the repository's official full-assessment reference.

## Evidence state

### Current DESE primary-source confirmed

The current DESE Grade-Level page links the governing **MAP Grade-Level Assessment Blueprints** resource. The current file is the April 2026 primary PDF named `MAP Grade-Level Assessment Blueprints_AOD.pdf`.

On 2026-08-23 the actual 15-page PDF was obtained and visually inspected page by page. The point ranges and reporting-category groupings below are therefore no longer a corroborating transcription target: they are directly confirmed against the current DESE primary file.

The primary PDF also confirms:

- Mathematics Grades 3-5 total 48 points; Grades 6-8 total 54 points.
- ELA Grades 3-6 and 8 total 56 points; Grade 7 totals 52 points.
- Science Grades 5 and 8 total 60 points.
- Grade 3-5 Math Performance Events are 6 points (13%).
- Grade 6-8 Math Performance Events are 8 points (15%).
- Assessments may contain SR, EBSR, CR, WT, PE, and/or TE items.

### Corrections made after direct primary-PDF inspection

The earlier corroborating copy had been interpreted too granularly in several Math rows. The current primary PDF groups some domains into one reporting category and gives one shared point range. These are material corrections:

- Grade 4 Math: **GM + DS is one 10-18 point category**, not separate GM 7-13 and DS 3-5 categories.
- Grade 5 Math: **GM + DS is one 8-18 point category**, not separate GM 6-14 and DS 2-4 categories.
- Grade 7 Math: **GM + DSP is one 9-18 point category**, not separate GM 4-8 and DSP 5-10 categories.
- Grade 8 Math: **NS + EEI is one 17-25 point category**, and **GM + DSP is one 12-20 point category**. The earlier split NS/EEI and GM/DSP ranges were not official reporting-category ranges.
- Grade 8 ELA: the 4-point "Approaching the task as a reader" row is under the **Language** strand/domain, not a second human-scored writing-task component.

These corrections are reflected in `js/blueprints.js` and the Math capacity/full-form diagnostics.

## Execution implications for this repository

Primary-current range confirmation is now closed, but that does **not** make the assessment blueprints executable or release-ready by itself.

1. **ELA** - complete operational forms still include Speaking/Listening contributions. Grades 4 and 8 additionally include a passage-based writing prompt that remains a deferred human-scored component. Supported auto-scored form construction must exclude only the genuinely deferred official components; it must not discard Grade 6/7 Writing-category selected-response/TE content simply because the reporting category is called Writing.
2. **Science** - the operational assessment includes constructed-response work in both sessions. The app now represents and stores manual CR practice, but the authoritative operational CR point/item allocation still has not been established and must not be guessed.
3. **Mathematics** - the blueprint assigns a fixed 6-point PE contribution in Grades 3-5 and 8 points in Grades 6-8. Current development PE bundles are operational-size, but the exact current-primary category grouping must be used when drawing and validating the ordinary portion of full forms.

## Promotion rule

An assessment can move from development practice toward complete-form release only after:

- the current DESE blueprint values are primary-source confirmed - **completed 2026-08-23**;
- bank tags are checked against the corrected official category model;
- every deferred component is explicitly represented in the product contract;
- executable form builders do not substitute auto-scored material for missing official components;
- the required 5,000 blueprint-constrained form and retake-pair audits pass on the exact candidate tree using these corrected ranges;
- independent clean-room bank review passes on the exact repaired candidate tree; and
- exact-tree CI/build and final UX/release checks pass.

Primary range verification is therefore a closed evidence gate, while `verified:false` and `executable:false` correctly remain in force until the remaining release blockers are closed.
