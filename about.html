About this project

This is an unofficial, original Missouri MAP Grade-Level practice application. It is designed to reproduce MAP session structure, calculator rules, untimed timing guidance, grade-level standards, and supported response styles without copying official secure or released question wording.

Current limitations

Audio/listening and human scoring are intentionally deferred. ELA listening is not yet playable, Grades 4 and 8 ELA long-form writing is not yet represented as release content, and written parts of Math performance events remain a fidelity limitation. Science practice can include constructed-response prompts: your written response is saved and shown for manual review, but the site does not automatically grade that prose or include those points in the automatic percentage.

No missing listening, writing, constructed-response scoring, or written performance-event component is replaced with extra multiple-choice questions and described as a complete operational MAP simulation.

Scoring

Results are raw practice points and strand summaries. Automatically scored points are reported separately from any written-response points that require human review. They are not official MAP scale scores or proficiency classifications.

Privacy

Practice state is stored locally in your browser. This static application has no account system or server-side student database.
