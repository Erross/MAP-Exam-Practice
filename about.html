About this project

This is an unofficial, original Missouri MAP Grade-Level practice application. It is designed to reproduce MAP session structure, calculator rules, untimed timing guidance, grade-level standards, and machine-scorable response styles without copying official secure or released question wording.

Current limitation

Audio/listening and responses requiring human scoring are intentionally deferred. That includes ELA listening, Grades 4 and 8 ELA writing prompts, Science constructed response, and written portions of Math performance events. The application does not substitute extra multiple-choice questions and pretend those components are present.

Scoring

Results are raw practice points and strand summaries. They are not official MAP scale scores or proficiency classifications.

Privacy

Practice state is stored locally in your browser. This static application has no account system or server-side student database.
